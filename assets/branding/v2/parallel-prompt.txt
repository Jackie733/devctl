Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: a single logo icon exploration for devctl, a local development multi-service process manager with a TUI.
Primary request: Create the "Parallel" direction: an abstract, extremely minimal, contemporary logo composed of exactly TWO parallel vertical bars, each interrupted once by a clear gap. The interruption is offset: the left bar gap is in its upper portion, and the right bar gap is in its lower portion. This creates exactly FOUR flat filled rectangular strokes in total, all with equal horizontal thickness. The two independent controlled service channels should feel compact and reliable, with generous distance between them. No letter d.
Input image 1 is a reference to the product's terminal layout and restrained utilitarian mood ONLY. Do not recreate the screen, any frames, terminal text, or UI.
Scene/backdrop: a uniform flat dark navy-black background #111827 across the entire 1024 by 1024 square image.
Composition: central symbol approximately 45% of canvas width and 60% of canvas height, with large clear margins. Exactly two vertical columns made from four filled rectangles. The columns share aligned top and bottom endpoints. Left column has a short upper rectangle, clear gap, long lower rectangle. Right column has long upper rectangle, clear gap, short lower rectangle. Gaps have equal height and occur at visibly different vertical positions. Straight clean edges with at most extremely subtle corner rounding.
Color palette: all four strokes are a single flat mint-white #C7F9DE.
Constraints: one clean standalone icon asset. Vector-like solid shapes. No gradients, no texture, no shadows, no 3D, no glow, no lettering or text. Do not add letters, code brackets, triangles, connecting lines, nodes, rings, outer frame, tile or backing plate. Do not make a collage. The visual has a basic ASCII companion "::", meaning two independent blocks in each column; the artwork itself contains no ASCII text.
